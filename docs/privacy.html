Privacy Policy – GlobeMetrics Automation

Last updated: 21 November 2026

GlobeMetrics Automation is used only by the owner of the GlobeMetrics YouTube channel to upload videos to their own channel through the YouTube Data API.

What data the app accesses

The app requests the youtube.upload permission. It uses it only to upload videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to the owner's channel. It does not read, collect or analyse any other Google or YouTube user data.

Storage

The OAuth access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's APIs.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
